# Areyos: How to Find Your Way

Areyos helps you find your way inside buildings like malls, hostels and offices. There's no app to download and no sign-up. All you need is your phone's browser.

---

## The journey at a glance

```
Scan the QR code  →  See where you are  →  Choose where to go
        →  Check the route  →  Follow the steps  →  You've arrived
```

---

## 1. Start: scan the QR code

Look for an **Areyos QR code** on a wall, pillar or sign near you.

- Tap **Scan QR code**, then point your camera at the code.
- **Camera not working?** Type the code printed under the QR (it looks like `AREYOS-2-F97386F5`) into the box and tap the arrow.

> Your phone may ask for permission to use the camera. Tap **Allow**. The camera is only used to read the code.

You'll briefly see **"Reading your QR code"** and then **"Loading your map"**.

---

## 2. See where you are

The map of your current floor opens.

- A **blue pulsing dot** marks **"You are here"**, the spot where you scanned the code.
- The top bar shows the **building name** and the **floor** you're on.
- A panel at the bottom shows your location and some popular places nearby. You can open or close this panel by tapping it.

**Moving around the map:**
| To do this | Do this |
|---|---|
| Zoom in or out | Pinch with two fingers, or double-tap |
| Move around | Drag with one finger |
| Go back to your location | Tap the **target** button |
| See the whole floor | Tap the **expand** button |

**Other floors:** use the floor buttons on the side of the map to look at another floor. Tapping a **lift or stairs** marker on the map also takes you to the floor it leads to.

---

## 3. Choose where to go

There are three ways to pick a destination:

1. **Search:** tap **Find a place** (or the search bar), then type a name such as "Washroom" or "Room 101".
2. **Suggestions:** tap one of the places listed in the bottom panel.
3. **Map:** tap any place marker directly on the map.

---

## 4. Check the route

Areyos works out the best route and shows a short summary:

- **Walk:** about how many minutes it will take
- **Floors:** whether you need to change floors, and how many times
- **Arrive:** your expected arrival time

The route is drawn on the map as a **blue line with moving arrows**. If the route goes through other floors, those floors are highlighted in the floor buttons.

- Tap **Start navigation** to begin.
- Tap **✕** to pick a different place.

---

## 5. Follow the steps

The top of the screen shows **"Navigating to"** and your destination.
A card at the bottom gives one instruction at a time, for example *"Turn left"* or *"Take the lift to First Floor"*.

- Tap **Next** after you complete each step.
- Tap **Back** to see the previous step again.
- When you need to change floors, the map switches to the next floor automatically.
- Tap **End** at the top to stop navigating at any time.

---

## 6. You've arrived

When you reach your destination, you'll see **"You've arrived"**. Tap **Done**.

You can then:
- **Scan code:** scan another QR code from a new spot
- **Find a place:** go somewhere else
- **Back to map:** keep looking at the map

---

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Something went wrong" | Tap **Try again**. If it still fails, tap **Use a different code**. |
| The QR code isn't recognised | Check the typed code for mistakes, or scan the code again. |
| "Loading floor plan…" won't go away | Check your internet connection and reload the page. |
| "No map is available for this floor yet" | The building hasn't added a map for this floor yet. Try another floor. |
| A route can't be found | That place may be closed right now. Choose another destination. |

---

## Tips

- **Scan a new code whenever you can.** It puts your location back exactly where you are.
- The app follows your phone's **light or dark mode** setting.
- **Your privacy:** you don't need an account, and nothing personal is collected.
